About Namevya

Connecting people with the stories, heritage, and meanings behind their names.

Our Mission

At Namevya, we believe every name holds a story. Names are deeply tied to our identities, lineages, cultural roots, and personal journeys. Our goal is to build the world's most accessible, accurate, and inspiring resource for discovering name meanings, pronunciations, etymologies, and historical contexts.

What We Offer

  • Rich Cultural History: Comprehensive background information on global naming traditions.
  • Etymology & Linguistics: In-depth root origins, linguistic variations, and accurate translations.
  • Pronunciation Guides: Audio and phonetic guidance across different languages and regions.
  • Related Names: Seamless discovery of derivative, similar, and sibling names.

Looking Ahead

Namevya is evolving into an interactive platform where users can explore personalized name suggestions, save favorites, and share naming traditions with a community of enthusiasts worldwide.
